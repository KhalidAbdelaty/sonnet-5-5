RIVERMARK SUBSCRIPTIONS - MONTH-END CLOSE POLICY (v3, effective 2026-01-01)

POL-1 Period. A month's close covers processor activity with a settled_date in that
calendar month (UTC). All amounts are integer cents, USD.

POL-2 Revenue. Subscription orders are recognized on order_date. Card charges settle
with the processor on the order date.

POL-3 Refunds. A refund is recognized in the month its settled_date falls in, whatever
the month of the original order.

POL-4 Processor costs. Fees the processor deducts are payment costs of the month they
settle in.

POL-5 Expected payout. Expected payout = orders recognized in the month - refunds
recognized in the month - processor costs of the month.

POL-6 Balanced. A month is reconciled when expected payout plus recorded adjustments
equals the processor payout to the cent.

POL-7 Corrections. Source exports are never edited. A correction is an adjustment
entry in the close adjustments ledger. Each entry names the policy rule it applies,
the source record that evidences it, and a signed amount applied to expected payout
(negative reduces expected payout). Finance approves the plan before entries are made.
